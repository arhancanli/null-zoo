\begin{abstract}
\noindent Corrections for backtest overfitting and data snooping---the deflated Sharpe ratio, the multiple-testing haircut, family-wise adjustments, White's Reality Check and Hansen's test for superior predictive ability (SPA)---are derived under assumptions that trading research violates. The Null Zoo is an open, versioned and pre-registered simulation benchmark that draws complete research searches (\nz{trials} strategies, \nz{obs} daily returns) from nine return families with known ground truth and scores \nz{v1.validator_count} validators by size and power, with \nz{reps.v1} searches per cell. Hansen's studentized SPA test, and Romano and Wolf's StepM with it, rejected \nz{both.size_range.spa_consistent}\% of skill-less searches at a nominal 5\%, while the unstudentized Reality Check rejected \nz{both.size_range.reality_check}\%; the pattern held on fresh seeds and in an independent second implementation. Pre-registered experiments trace the excess to the long-run variance estimate the bootstrap holds fixed, compounded under negative skew by the dependence between a mean and its estimated standard deviation: re-studentizing every resample restores calibration (\nz{v2.A.size_range_nonar.spa_c_boot_t}\% outside the autocorrelated family), while the excess shrinks with the sample, grows with the number of strategies and reaches \nz{v2.A.size.spa_c_b22.skew_negative}\% at the block length the Python package arch uses by default. arch's own SPA test is unaffected only because its \texttt{studentize} option does nothing. Where volatilities differ or most strategies are poor, a correctly studentized and recentred test is far more powerful than the Reality Check; a floored statistic with a strict inequality can reject when no strategy beats the benchmark. A lag-one autocorrelation of 0.2 raises the size of the single-series tests that ignore it to about \nz{v1.size.luck_trials.ar1}\%, and the deflated Sharpe ratio's 0.95 rule misses \nz{v1.dsr_fail_to_accept.iid_normal}\% of searches containing a strategy with a true Sharpe ratio of 2. \nz{v2.predictions.held_count} of \nz{v2.predictions.count} pre-registered predictions held; all results reproduce from the released code.
\end{abstract}
